Who you'd be working with

One person, writing every line himself, start to finish.

I'm a freelance Roblox game developer working mainly as a scripter. I take on contract work for studios and build my own games on the side, so I've seen both what a client needs on a deadline and what a codebase needs to survive a year of updates.

Most of my work is backend: entity and combat systems, player data with versioned migrations, buffered networking, server-side validation and anti-exploit checks. I also do a lot of UI programming, usually reactive UI with full animation passes.

I work inside your existing framework rather than replacing it. If you have conventions, I follow them. If you don't, I'll set up a clean structure of services, controllers and shared modules that your other scripters can pick up quickly.

--// Process

How a commission runs

The same six steps for every project, so you always know what's next and what you've paid for.

  1. Brief

    You message me with what you need, your deadline and any existing code or framework I should work in.

  2. Quote

    I send a written quote in USD with the scope, the milestones and an estimated timeline. Nothing is paid until you agree to it.

  3. Invoice

    I send a PayPal invoice for the first milestone. Work starts once it's paid.

  4. Build

    I work in your place through Team Create or Rojo and post progress clips as each part comes together.

  5. Review

    You test the milestone in Studio. I fix anything that doesn't match the agreed scope before we move on.

  6. Handover

    The finished code stays in your place. I walk your team through how it's laid out and stay available for follow-up fixes.

--// Terms

Refunds, ownership & the fine print

These apply to every commission unless we agree on something different in writing.

Refunds

  • If you cancel before I start a paid milestone, you get that payment back in full.
  • If I can't finish a milestone you've paid for, you get a full refund for it.
  • Once a milestone is delivered and you've approved it, its payment isn't refundable.
  • For hourly work, hours already worked are billed; nothing is charged in advance.

Revisions & support

  • Bugs in delivered work that fall within the agreed scope are fixed for free for 14 days after handover.
  • New features or changes to the scope are quoted separately.
  • Bugs caused by later edits from other developers aren't covered, but I can fix them hourly.

Ownership

  • When a project is paid in full, you own the code I wrote for it and can use or change it however you like.
  • General-purpose tools I wrote before your project may be reused in other work; you get a permanent licence to use them in your game.
  • I may show short clips of finished work in my portfolio unless you ask me not to.

Confidentiality

  • I don't share your code, place files, designs or plans with anyone.
  • I'm happy to sign an NDA before seeing your project.
  • Access to your place is only used for the work you hired me for.

Communication

  • I work in Pacific Time and usually reply within one day.
  • Progress updates go to your Discord or email as each part of the work comes together.
  • If something will run late, you'll hear about it before the deadline, not after.

Disputes

  • If you're unhappy with anything, message me first. Most problems are sorted out in a day.
  • If we can't agree, PayPal's dispute process applies to the invoice in question.
